Roadmap

What is being built

Done

Story Bible

The canonical source of truth — worldbuilding, laws, environmental and character codices, the full chapter treatment across the three books, and the reference sections. Every other document and the whole website derive from it.

Under way

Manuscript drafting

Book 1, Fractured Horizons, covers Acts 1 to 3; Book 2, Resonant Faultlines, picks up at Act 4; Book 3, Harmonic Worlds, closes the arc. A chapter counts as drafted only once its prose is locked and registered as manuscript quality — everything before that stage exists as a full treatment in the Story Bible.

41 of 90 chapters are drafted to manuscript quality and registered as such.

Done

Website

A single generated site built from the Story Bible's extracted JSON and the manuscript markdown. Manuscript chapters get their own reading pages, distinct from their Story Bible treatments, and the homepage carries the project's working state — the roadmap, the decisions ledger and open items — the way Studio once did before the two merged. Published to GitHub Pages at wallabynio.github.io/meridian-resonance as plain HTML, kept out of search indexes with noindex and a blanket robots.txt disallow. Rebuilding is not publishing: a rebuild writes into Deploy/ and stops there, and uploading happens only when asked. See Publishing Workflow.md.
