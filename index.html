A Hard Science-Fiction Trilogy · 2087

Empathy is not an emotion.
It is a physical law.

On a world rebuilt from climate collapse and an AI uprising, something beneath the lunar colony has begun to answer — a force that responds not to machines, but to the quality of human attention. As three powers race to study it, weaponise it, or protect it, the people caught between them must decide what consciousness is worth.
