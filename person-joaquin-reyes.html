What the prose has shown

look

Ch 05Near sixty, grey-haired, brown eyes, a lean lined face, a day's beard, the camel coat; carries his keys on a ring round one finger and turns them.

dress

Ch 12Joaquín takes his coat off at thirty-four degrees, to applause.

Ch 24On go-live night Joaquín takes his camel coat off, over the back of a chair, for the second time in twelve years; his shirt sticks in a dark stripe down his spine.

Ch 09By the floor's rule his camel coat comes off when the street reaches thirty-four, applauded; on 22 March 2096 it comes off in March, an hour into the morning block (In March it's an accident), the night lead's Three in a year. He kept it on for twelve.

Ch 13On 10 April 2096, eleven degrees in the street, the camel coat is back on; he runs a book in grease pencil on the flood glass on which south-tip lobby dries first after 2 April (Number four has a man with a fan); Rachel has bet on the glass and never lost, and never paid him.

body

Ch 04Joaquín is sixty-one; his knees "were in the Evacuations".

Ch 01A Gate operator in chair four, the thrusters; at 14:58 his clock falls thirty-one to twenty-six to twenty-two and he says Primo, Rosa's word for him since they were kids; lifted out at nineteen, fogged, one hand won't keep still; My head's been demoted.

Ch 16On 2 May 2096, mid-block, his key ring stops turning on his finger while his bleed clock reads twenty-seven and green and the voice calls Chair four. Edge holding. Leave him. He's doing beautifully.; he doesn't answer Kaelin's two taps; Kaelin pulls him out of the frame sideways, the head cradle letting go like a seal parting; the third burn fires on time and logs 13:59. CUATRO. Burn three. Clean.; gone eight minutes, he comes up asking for his keys (I'm very good when I'm not there.).

sound

Ch 06Mid-shift on 2 March, eyes shut in chair four, says Aur'k's seam call on chair one a breath before Aur'k, in his phrasing (Boundary. Chair one, edge holding. Lift at fifteen-oh-five.); his edge shows nothing but Joaquín; afterwards: Everybody does your voice. Moss does it better than you. She does the pause.; wants a sandwich; will tell his aunt fourteen minutes for two flans.

habit

Ch 12Joaquín calls the dome day a wet-bulb Monday, and for once doesn't follow it with a joke.

Ch 16Joaquín opens June's surge pool (REYES 2.40) and Rachel never bets; the surge: 3 June night tide, one metre forty over the walk-line on the south boardwalks of the Shallows, 23:10 to 01:30, 406 households, barrier holds; the sitting held at the Battery, seven at the table

Ch 21Joaquín has come into Rachel's room without knocking once before in twelve years; there is a family baptism coming; he smiles that way only before bad news.

Ch 27Reads the files out from the rail; plays along unasked, holding his thumb on the printer and misprinting the pass, Vamos, viejo; says nothing when she takes it, mouth pressed shut.

Ch 30At Rosa's funeral in white, with no coat; She wanted the sea; She'll be a plane tree. She'll hate it; Ay, prima, to the trees, and smiles.

Ch 09On 22 March 2096 at half past nine he goes quiet at his aunt's table mid-sentence, fork in hand, eyes open, breathing; Rachel holds his hand without calling the medic; at 23:41 he comes back (Jefa. Did I miss the pass?), asks where his coat is, and doesn't remember taking it off; drinks two glasses of water and eats the rice cold; walks her down in his socks: Same chair tomorrow?

Ch 10Holds his handheld at arm's length rather than admit he needs lenses.

Ch 10On 28 March 2096 Aur'k finds a smooth place in his strand, neither dark nor warm, where Thursday afternoon should be; he holds the burns right up to it; doesn't remember taking his coat off (Twelve years I wait for thirty-four in March); asks what the Malé pass was like.
