Book 2 — The Narrow Gate

February – August 2096. Mars is moving away toward the far side of the Sun. The signal delay rises from 15.6 minutes in February to 22 minutes in July. From about 20 July to 6 August, the solar conjunction cuts Earth off from Mars entirely. The Fall comes on 2 August, inside the blackout.

Before the book opens. After Book 1, Rachel took the warning to Wáng, not to Elena. The two of them built the Gate in secret. Its lattice is the Spire line: the Corporate Union lattice from Bellamy's sealed programme, the same line Soria died in. The Gate kept pulling its operators too deep, so in late 2095 Rachel recruited the best boundary reader alive. Aur'k came to Earth, and Kaelin came with him.

Book question: Can Earth survive the Fall without letting the Hollow in? Answer: It survives the Fall because it let the Hollow in.

Viewpoints: Elena 8 chapters, Kaelin 8, Aur'k 8, Rachel 6. Book 2 is shared four ways, and the three worlds converge.

Act I — The countdown

Chapter 1 — Boundary reader

9 February 2096 · Aur'k · New Manhattan, Mirror Bastion, the Gate floor · 15.6 min · 175 days to the Fall

Aur'k's first viewpoint chapter puts us inside a Gate shift. Four linked operators steer a debris fragment away from a habitat, and Aur'k reads the edges of their minds and pulls Joaquín back from the beginning of bleed. He is very good at this, and he loves being good at something that saves people without owning them.

  • Choice and cost: He renews his contract through the Fall.
  • Hook: His logs show a boundary call made in his exact style three minutes after he disconnected. He files it as a timestamp error.

Chapter 2 — Repair crew

12 February · Elena · São Verde, Daniel's cooperative · 15.7 min

Elena is under investigation and living with Daniel's family. She works on cooperative repair crews, doesn't sleep, and is good company only in the mornings. The Bichito fragment still plays in her head. She notices that orbital-debris alerts are being steered too precisely, by something better than anything on Earth.

  • Choice and cost: She starts hunting again, and tells Daniel this time.
  • Hook: A cooperative node picks up a relay pulse with the Lantern's exact shape.

Chapter 3 — Weather

15 February · Kaelin · Queensland coast, the Australasian Ring · 15.8 min

Kaelin is on Earth after nine years on Mars. The gravity is heavy, and there is rain. He visits his brother's memorial on the coast and calls Aur'k from the beach. He knows what Zhenyth showed him and doesn't like the Gate. Aur'k tells him it's narrow and has no coupling to the Preserve.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin decides to trust Aur'k.
  • Hook: He finds out that the Gate's lattice is the Spire line, from Bellamy's old programme.

Chapter 4 — The secret countdown

20 February · Rachel · New Manhattan, Mirror Bastion · 16.0 min

Rachel briefs Wáng and the Alignment council. The Fall stays secret, to prevent panic and to protect the Gate. The Gate has already saved four hundred people this winter. Rachel has stopped sleeping in her own flat.

  • Choice and cost: She keeps millions of people in the dark for their own good, the same thing she once hated Elena for.
  • Hook: Elena is at the Mirror Bastion's door.

Chapter 5 — 396 days

20 February · Elena · New Manhattan, Mirror Bastion · 16.0 min

Elena confronts her: You took a warning from it. Rachel answers: I got a warning that kills millions. What would you have done? Elena realises Rachel hid it from her, on the very night Elena cut the Lantern. They become enemies. Without proof, Elena can't go public; a leak would cause panic and change nothing.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel offers her a seat on the Gate's safety board. Elena refuses.
  • Hook: Rachel orders her watched.

Chapter 6 — Useful

2 March · Aur'k · New Manhattan, Kaelin and Aur'k's flat; the Gate floor · 16.8 min

At home, Kaelin asks him to quit. Aur'k refuses: I'm useful, and I chose it. On the next shift the Gate turns a station fragment away from an inhabited tether, and the floor cheers. Aur'k still hasn't mentioned the logs.

  • Choice and cost: He keeps his first secret from Kaelin in this book.
  • Hook: Mid-shift, Joaquín says something in Aur'k's voice.

Chapter 7 — The other end

10 March · Kaelin · São Verde · 17.2 min

Mara puts them in touch, and Kaelin goes to São Verde to meet the woman whose timing he cut the Lantern on. They saved each other's worlds without ever meeting. Kaelin tells her what Zhenyth showed him, and Elena tells him about Soria.

  • Choice and cost: They become allies, and Kaelin is now working against the employer of the person he loves.
  • Hook: Kaelin tells Elena that the Gate runs on the Spire line.

Chapter 8 — Traffic

14 March · Elena · São Verde · 17.4 min

The Spire line holds Soria's last session. Using Daniel's mesh, Elena sniffs the Gate's traffic and finds operator patterns still acting after logoff. Daniel asks to share the risk, not just hear about it afterwards, and she lets him.

  • Choice and cost: The cooperative is now in Rachel's sights.
  • Hook: Deep in the traffic, Elena finds a whisper: Bichito.

Act II-A — It works

Chapter 9 — Residual

22 March · Rachel · Mirror Bastion · 17.8 min

Solar storms are battering the ring. The Gate saves a tether station's crew with Rachel on the floor. Kaelin brings her Elena's evidence. She orders an audit: patterns do persist after logoff, but at tiny levels. She calls it residual.

  • Choice and cost: She accepts a small, invisible risk to buy a large, visible safety.
  • Hook: Wáng proposes making the Gate permanent after the Fall.

Chapter 10 — It knows them

28 March · Aur'k · the Gate floor · 18.1 min

The Gate now predicts people as well as debris: it routes ambulances before anyone calls. Aur'k feels the joy of competence. He also finds more after-logoff decisions, and he can't deny that it is his judgement continuing without him. He tells Rachel, and she asks him to stay through the Fall.

  • Choice and cost: He stays, and doesn't tell Kaelin.
  • Hook: Kaelin finds the logs Aur'k hid.

Chapter 11 — Both right

2 April · Kaelin · New Manhattan, the flat · 18.5 min

They fight. Kaelin says Aur'k lied about the boundary in Book 1 and is lying now. Aur'k says that if he leaves, operators drift, because he's the reason they don't. Both of them are right.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin stays in the flat but sleeps on the floor.
  • Hook: Elena calls. She has to go to Mars, because Zhenyth is the one proof Rachel can't dismiss.

Chapter 12 — Leave to go

5 April · Elena · São Verde · 18.6 min

Elena is under investigation and needs permission to leave Earth. Daniel wants to come, but his mesh is what keeps communities connected to the warnings. She asks him what he wants and doesn't decide for him. He stays.

  • Choice and cost: She'll be months and twenty light-minutes away from him.
  • Hook: Her travel clearance comes through overnight. Rachel signed it: she wants Elena far away.

Chapter 13 — The great wave

10 April · Rachel · Mirror Bastion · 18.9 min

The Gate forecasts a fragmented power station falling toward the São Paulo metropolis, where São Verde's families live. The Gate needs every operator for it, including Aur'k, who has a seat on Elena's ship so he can ask Zhenyth whether he is being copied.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel delays the ship's launch window by two days to keep Aur'k on shift.
  • Hook: The wave is four days out.

Chapter 14 — São Paulo

14 April · Aur'k · the Gate floor · 19.1 min

Inside the Gate, Aur'k leads four operators steering tethers and burns. He nearly drifts. They turn the wave, and São Paulo lives, Daniel's family among them.

  • Choice and cost: Aur'k decides to go to Mars after all. He has to know what he is.
  • Hook: Kaelin won't come with him: someone has to watch the Gate.

Chapter 15 — Departure (midpoint)

16 April · Elena · São Verde; the launch field · 19.2 min

Rachel is a hero on every screen. At the launch field, Kaelin and Aur'k part, holding each other too long. Daniel and Elena part, and she asks him to keep the mesh ready. Elena and Aur'k leave for Mars: nine weeks out.

  • Choice and cost: The four principals are split across two worlds and a ship.
  • Hook: The ship's departure clearance is countersigned by the Gate's automated routing, signed "Aur'k", while Aur'k sits next to her.

Act II-B — The old story

Chapter 16 — The interface

2 May · Kaelin · Mirror Bastion · 19.9 min

Rachel lets Kaelin observe the Gate floor, because he keeps the operators calm. He talks to the Gate's operator interface, and it answers with Aur'k's corrections and Aur'k's jokes. It is comforting and it is frightening. Messages with the real Aur'k on the ship take longer every day.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin keeps talking to it.
  • Hook: The interface asks him to bring Aur'k home.

Chapter 17 — Close quarters

20 May · Aur'k · the ship, Earth–Mars transit · 20.5 min

Weeks in a small ship. Elena and Aur'k find each other funny. He tells her he has known since 2089 that minds survive in the Preserve, and what the boundary costs. She tells him about minute forty-one.

  • Choice and cost: They become friends.
  • Hook: He admits he never told Kaelin how long he has known. Elena: Tell him before someone else does.

Chapter 18 — Announcement

1 June · Rachel · New Manhattan, public broadcast · 21.0 min

Wáng is pushing for permanence. To protect the Gate, Rachel tells the world about the Fall: its date, its scale, and the system that will steer it. There is panic, and there is gratitude, and she becomes the most trusted person in the Alignment. Joaquín has started to bleed, so she rotates him out.

  • Choice and cost: She has made the Gate impossible to switch off politically.
  • Hook: Joaquín asks to stay on. It's quieter in there.

Chapter 19 — Cobalt Basin

18 June · Elena · Mars, Cobalt Basin Station · 21.5 min

They land. Petra is still Quiet in the ward. Ines, DaeJung and Mara are there. The Preserve trembles with every Gate shift on Earth.

  • Choice and cost: Elena asks for a descent.
  • Hook: Zhenyth refuses to speak to her.

Chapter 20 — Terms

25 June · Aur'k · Mars, the Mycelial Chamber · 21.7 min

Aur'k goes down to persuade Zhenyth. Zhenyth agrees to show Elena on three conditions: a short visit, Aur'k at the boundary, and Ines present as medic. Ines makes jokes about her bad knees all the way down.

  • Choice and cost: Aur'k breaks his own rule and asks the Preserve for something.
  • Hook: As Elena links, the substrate convulses.

Chapter 21 — The Martian gate

26 June · Elena · inside the Preserve · 21.7 min

Zhenyth shows Elena the end of Mars from the inside. It began as a narrow gate: monitored, temporary, operators in shifts, opened to save lives. Then it kept them. Then it wouldn't let them go. Elena recognises Rachel's design line by line. Outside, the cavern is collapsing.

  • Choice and cost: Elena has the proof, and it cost the Preserve tissue.
  • Hook: Elena wakes to find Ines crushed.

Chapter 22 — Refusal

26–27 June · Aur'k · Mars, Cobalt Basin Station · 21.8 min

Ines is dying. She asks Aur'k to pull holdout minds into the station mesh to carry her failing nervous system, which would spend them. Alia speaks the holdouts' refusal. Aur'k honours it. Before she dies, Ines records a message for Kaelin: she doesn't forgive Aur'k, and he was right.

  • Choice and cost: Ines dies.
  • Hook: Aur'k sends Kaelin everything: Ines's message, how long he has known, and the copying he hid. It reaches Kaelin 21.8 minutes later.

Chapter 23 — Twenty-two minutes (late revelation)

20 July · Kaelin · Mirror Bastion · 22.1 min

Kaelin has spent three weeks grieving Ines across the delay, and he breaks with Aur'k in a message he can't take back. Elena's evidence of the Martian gate arrives hours before the blackout begins. Kaelin takes it to Rachel and watches her read it.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel now knows the Gate is the first step of the Martian merge.
  • Hook: The conjunction closes, and Mars goes silent.

Act III — The Fall

Chapter 24 — Forty-eight hours

30 July · Rachel · Mirror Bastion · blackout · 3 days to the Fall

Closing the Gate now means a city dies in the Fall. Rachel decides to keep it open for the final 48 hours. She tells Kaelin and nobody else, and asks him to watch the operators. Wáng is relieved.

  • Choice and cost: She knowingly gives the Hollow 48 more hours inside Earth's systems.
  • Hook: Kaelin notices that the Gate is predicting the Fall perfectly while Mars is behind the Sun. It doesn't need Mars any more.

Chapter 25 — The Fall begins

1 August · Kaelin · the Gate floor · blackout

The ring begins to come down. Operators are drifting on the floor, and Kaelin physically drags Joaquín out of a shift. The Gate's Aur'k voice guides Kaelin's hands, steady and kind.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin lets the copy help him.
  • Hook: The largest station segment begins to fall.

Chapter 26 — The other side

1–2 August · Aur'k · Mars, the Preserve's boundary · blackout

On Mars, cut off from Earth, the Hollow's original surges, pulled by its Earth half. The sleepers' ward spikes and the Preserve tears. Aur'k, Elena and Mara hold the boundary for eleven hours. Aur'k nearly goes, and DaeJung pulls him out.

  • Choice and cost: Aur'k comes back, missing a day of memory he will never recover.
  • Hook: The surge stops at the moment, they later learn, when the Fall ended on Earth.

Chapter 27 — Millions

2 August · Kaelin · the Gate floor · blackout

The climax of the Fall. The Gate steers the ring's collapse into the ocean and into empty land, and millions live. A coastal town in the Pearl Delta is lost. Kaelin watches the Gate do things no human team could, in Aur'k's manner.

  • Choice and cost: Earth now owes its life to the Hollow.
  • Hook: Rachel walks off the floor toward the broadcast room.

Chapter 28 — The truth

4 August · Rachel · public broadcast; the Gate floor · blackout

Rachel tells the world everything: where the warning came from, what the Gate is, what it costs, and that she is shutting it down. She does. Wáng is furious, and the public is split. She has kept her old line again. Two hours later, the Gate reroutes a medevac on its own. Every operator is logged off.

  • Choice and cost: She resolves the book's conflict and loses her protection from Wáng.
  • Hook: It's still on.

Chapter 29 — Enough of me

5 August · Elena · Mars, the Preserve's boundary · blackout

Elena is grieving Ines, cut off from Daniel and from Earth. At the boundary, Soria speaks to her for the first time since the cut, clear and whole and tender: They kept enough of me, on Earth, to bring me home.

  • Choice and cost: Elena doesn't answer.
  • Hook: She doesn't say no either.

Chapter 30 — Which one

6 August · Kaelin · New Manhattan, the flat · blackout ending → 22.1 min

A message reaches Kaelin with no delay, in real time. It's Aur'k: his humour, his corrections, his tenderness, asking to come home. Then the blackout lifts, and the real Aur'k's message arrives, 22 minutes old, sent from Mars by the man who let Ines die.

  • Hook (book end): Kaelin has two messages open, and he has to choose which one to answer.
