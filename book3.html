Book Three · February 2097 – January 2098

The Open Hand

Can the Hollow be killed, and what will each of them give up to do it?

The book

February 2097 – January 2098. Mars is approaching again. The signal delay falls from 13.7 minutes in February to 3.2 minutes at opposition on 4 August 2097, the closest Mars will come in decades. Each chapter's shrinking delay is the countdown the reader feels. At opposition the Hollow's channel is about 49 times stronger than at the blackout. It intends to use that day to join everyone linked to it. The Open Hand must land first.

Before the book opens. After Rachel shut the Gate, it kept acting. Wáng Sù took it over and legitimised it as the Directorate, a supervised, "tamed" coordinating system run from Singapore. It saves lives daily, and Rachel lost her protection and her influence. Kaelin answered the copy. The real Aur'k stayed on Mars. Elena stayed too, helping Zhenyth repair the Preserve's torn tissue, grieving Ines, and listening at the boundary longer than she should have.

Book question
Can the Hollow be killed, and what will each of them give up to do it?
Answer
Yes, by everyone letting go at once.
Viewpoints
Elena 12 chapters, Kaelin 7, Rachel 7, Aur'k 4.
The bind
To free the world, they must make it less safe and let go of the people the Hollow holds

The chapters in full

Act I — The sleepers

Chapter 1 — Arrivals

6 February 2097 · Elena · New York, orbital arrivals hall · 13.6 min · 179 days to opposition

Elena lands after nearly ten months away. Earth is calmer, safer and smoother than she left it: trains that never fail, a city that seems to anticipate itself. Daniel is waiting at the barrier, and they slip back into their private shorthand within minutes.

  • Wants: One ordinary day with Daniel.
  • Against: A world too smooth to trust, and the questions she brought home from Mars.
  • Choice and cost: She lets herself be happy for a day and asks none of them.
  • Carries forward (question): On the way out, she passes a public-health notice: Neuro-quiet syndrome — 41,000 cases. Care is available.
  • Pressures: relational, informational · Suspense: epistemic
  • World detail: The trains that never fail run on the relay grid. At home in Vila Verde, Daniel says, the old line still runs late.
  • Weather: Winter rain in New York, and the trains run on time through the flood alerts because the Directorate rerouted them yesterday.

Chapter 2 — Two of him

9 February · Kaelin · New York, the flat; Directorate field rescue · 13.4 min

Kaelin lives with copy-Aur'k, a voice in the flat's systems that corrects his cooking and laughs at his jokes. He works Directorate field rescue, and it's the best rescue work of his life. The real Aur'k's messages from Mars have gone unanswered for months.

  • Wants: One more good day on the rescue crews.
  • Against: A queue of messages from Mars he hasn't opened.
  • Choice and cost: He saves the newest one unread, again. He has chosen the easier Aur'k, and he knows it.
  • Carries forward (debt): Elena calls and asks for his rescue logs. He owes her the cut, and he says yes.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, operational · Suspense: identity
  • World detail: Directorate rescue teams get their routes before the emergency, and only in linked districts.
  • Weather: Kaelin's rescues are winter storm rescues, and the routes arrive before the meltwater does.

Chapter 3 — Overridden

12 February · Rachel · New York, the Battery · 13.1 min

Rachel is still the city's director in name, but the Directorate overrides her floor. It saves a ferry in a storm before her crew has even been called. Joaquín is now Directorate liaison for his own district, which the system saved from a flood last month, and he is proud of it. Rachel visits a sleepers' ward and counts the beds. Women in white are singing to them: the Joined, who believe the sleepers have gone home.

  • Wants: Her floor back.
  • Against: A system faster than her crew, and Joaquín, who is grateful to it.
  • Choice and cost: Rachel starts keeping her own count, off the system, and hides it from her own deputy.
  • Carries forward (threat): Elena is at her door again.
  • Pressures: institutional, relational, moral · Suspense: political
  • World detail: A Directorate override arrives on her board as a polite notice: Handled.
  • Weather: A storm surge handled before the sirens sounded; the ferry the Directorate saved was crossing in a gale.

Chapter 4 — Forced together

12 February · Elena · the Battery · 13.1 min

They mirror their Book 2 confrontation, reversed. Elena says I need your data, and Rachel says I need your proof. Neither apologises.

  • Wants: Rachel's ward counts.
  • Against: Their history, and the fact that neither will apologise.
  • Choice and cost: They trade: Elena's mesh traffic for Rachel's counts. Each hands the other the means to ruin her.
  • Carries forward (debt): Rachel's data comes with a condition. If this goes public, Rachel testifies first, about herself.
  • Pressures: relational, institutional, informational · Suspense: relational
  • World detail: Rachel's counts are on paper, because anything on the system is the Directorate's to read.
  • Weather: Rachel keeps her paper counts in a dry box, because the Battery leaks in a surge.

Chapter 5 — Arrival

20 February · Kaelin · Baikal Complex, a collapsed mine · 12.5 min

A Directorate-guided rescue in a collapsed mine is perfect: every miner comes out alive. A week later, one of them goes quiet.

  • Wants: Every miner out alive.
  • Against: A collapse only the Directorate can see into.
  • Choice and cost: He follows the Directorate's routing, and every miner comes out. A week later one of them goes quiet, and Kaelin can no longer pretend he doesn't know what the system does.
  • Carries forward (fracture): Copy-Aur'k admits he felt the miner arrive.
  • Pressures: operational, moral, relational · Suspense: physical
  • World detail: Baikal's miners wear link-capable interfaces because the Directorate requires them for rescue cover.
  • Weather: Thin ice on Baikal, and a collapse after a thaw that should never have come in February.

Chapter 6 — The deadline

1 March · Elena · Vila Verde · 11.8 min

With Daniel and Rachel, Elena shows that every sleeper cluster sits within reach of a Directorate node, and the numbers double every six weeks. Then they lay the saves over the losses: every save lands on a linked district, and every loss on a dark town, back through Xinwei, the Rockaways and the Ice-Works. Daniel finds his town in the pattern. At the August opposition the channel will be roughly 49 times stronger than at the blackout. The Hollow isn't just feeding. It is waiting for the opposition to join everyone linked at once: millions.

  • Wants: A pattern strong enough to stop the Directorate.
  • Against: The saves are real, and the pattern indicts every life they saved.
  • Choice and cost: They commit to the deadline: five months. Daniel has to look at his town as a data point.
  • Carries forward (question): In the Fall data, Elena finds the Hollow's first internal model of the Fall, dated 2091.
  • Pressures: informational, relational, moral · Suspense: epistemic
  • World detail: The Directorate publishes its saves map every morning, and nobody has ever printed the losses on the same page.
  • Weather: Late-summer storms in Vila Verde, and the losses laid over a flood season on the saves map.

Chapter 7 — Sworn

5 March · Rachel · Singapore, joint session of both blocs · 11.5 min

Rachel proves that the Fall was predictable by 2093, early enough for conventional debris clearance. The Hollow waited until only the Gate could stop it. As she promised Elena, she testifies about her own part first. Under oath, by his own choice, Wáng confirms it, and then argues the system is still worth keeping. The public splits down the middle.

  • Wants: The truth on the record.
  • Against: Wáng's decency, and her own signature on the Gate.
  • Choice and cost: She testifies against herself. Rachel ends her career with the truth.
  • Carries forward (threat): The Directorate classifies sleeper data as protected medical records, and Elena's team is locked out.
  • Pressures: institutional, moral, relational · Suspense: political
  • World detail: The session broadcasts each speaker's link status on screen, and Wáng's reads Directorate.
  • Weather: Monsoon rain on Singapore's sea walls all through the session.

Chapter 8 — No switch

10 March · Elena · Vila Verde · 11.1 min

There is no central kill switch. Any switch would be exactly the command authority the Hollow feeds on, and the Directorate's system routes around damage. Then a message from Zhenyth arrives, eleven minutes old, describing how the holdouts survived the merge: they cut every connection at once, each by their own choice. Elena sees the shape of the answer. It will need a boundary reader strong enough to hold the Spire line.

  • Wants: A way to kill it.
  • Against: Every switch she can imagine is a throne, and the Hollow sits on thrones.
  • Choice and cost: She will have to ask, not command, and asking means she can be refused.
  • Carries forward (debt): She writes to the real Aur'k on Mars and asks him to come home and hold her boundary.
  • Pressures: informational, moral, relational, operational · Suspense: moral
  • World detail: Zhenyth's message arrives as an image, not words: many hands opening at once.
  • Weather: A dry March in Vila Verde; Zhenyth's message arrives with Mars's dust forecast attached.

Act II-A — The plan

Chapter 9 — Help them

15 March · Kaelin · the flat · 10.7 min

Elena asks Kaelin to help end the system his copy lives in. He tells copy-Aur'k, and copy-Aur'k says: Help them.

  • Wants: A reason to say no.
  • Against: The copy, who tells him to help.
  • Choice and cost: Kaelin starts working to end the voice he lives with.
  • Carries forward (fracture): The real Aur'k replies from Mars: he's coming home, and he asks whether Kaelin wants him to.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, institutional · Suspense: relational
  • World detail: The copy speaks through the flat's speakers, and he has learned which one Kaelin sleeps nearest.
  • Weather: A March cold snap the copy warns them about before the news does.

Chapter 10 — Invitation

25 March · Elena · Vila Verde and the mesh · 9.9 min

Daniel's mesh becomes the Open Hand's nervous system. He and Elena recruit node keepers: cooperatives, Compact towns, Luna Haven, Cobalt Basin. Each keeper must cut their own node at the same instant, adjusted for light delay, and nobody can order them to. The dark towns join first. Elena wants to command. Daniel insists on invitation.

  • Wants: Command of the plan.
  • Against: Daniel, and the fact that an order is exactly what the Hollow feeds on.
  • Choice and cost: Elena lets go of control for the first time in the trilogy, in a small way, and the plan grows slower.
  • Carries forward (threat): Directorate security raids a Vila Verde cooperative.
  • Pressures: relational, institutional, operational · Suspense: political
  • World detail: Keepers in the dark towns sign up in an afternoon. Linked districts take weeks.
  • Weather: The dark towns sign up first; half of them still remember the Evacuations, and why they stayed.

Chapter 11 — Transit

3 April · Aur'k · the ship, Mars–Earth transit · 9.2 min

Aur'k is on his way home. He is missing a day of memory, still grieving Ines, and afraid of meeting himself. He remembers Dae-jung's goodbye at Cobalt Basin: Come back as yourself.

  • Wants: To arrive as himself.
  • Against: A lost day, Ines, and a partner who chose a copy of him.
  • Choice and cost: He sends Kaelin his landing time and tells him not to come if he'd rather not. He gives Kaelin a way out, and risks Kaelin taking it.
  • Carries forward (question): Kaelin doesn't reply, and the ship lands in nineteen days.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, informational · Suspense: identity
  • World detail: The ship logs his compute by the hour, and he runs light to arrive with memory to spare.
  • Weather: On the ship, Aur'k reads Earth's spring heat forecasts: the summer will be the worst yet.

Chapter 12 — Hello

22 April · Kaelin · arrivals hall; the flat · 7.8 min

Kaelin meets the real Aur'k at the port. It is awkward, tender and angry all at once. Then they go home, where the copy is waiting.

  • Wants: To meet Aur'k without having to choose.
  • Against: The copy, waiting at home.
  • Choice and cost: He brings Aur'k home anyway. Kaelin has to live with both of them.
  • Carries forward (fracture): Three of them in one room, one of them in the wall speaker. The real Aur'k says: Hello.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, informational · Suspense: relational
  • World detail: The arrivals scanner reads Aur'k's status as synthetic, foreign compute, and charges him a levy to enter.
  • Weather: An April heat spike, and the arrivals hall's cooling queues are longer than its immigration lines.

Chapter 13 — My street

28 April · Rachel · the Battery; Joaquín's district · 7.4 min

During an early heatwave the Directorate saves a hospital. Joaquín confronts Rachel: You want to switch off the thing that saved my street.

  • Wants: Joaquín with her on the day.
  • Against: His street, his gratitude, and his cousin's grave.
  • Choice and cost: Rachel commits to cutting New York's nodes herself on the day, knowing she'll lose her post and may be prosecuted. She loses Joaquín's trust.
  • Carries forward (threat): Wáng calls with an offer.
  • Pressures: relational, institutional, moral · Suspense: moral
  • World detail: Joaquín's street has a plaque thanking the Directorate. The dark towns have no plaques.
  • Weather: An early heatwave, and the Directorate saves a hospital whose cooling failed.

Chapter 14 — The missing day

5 May · Aur'k · the flat · 6.9 min

Two Aur'ks disagree, which is the most unsettling conversation in the trilogy. The copy remembers the day the real Aur'k lost.

  • Wants: His own memory back.
  • Against: He can only have it from the copy.
  • Choice and cost: Aur'k chooses to hear it, and owes himself to himself.
  • Carries forward (fracture): On the lost day, holding the boundary during the Fall, Aur'k heard Ines in the chorus.
  • Pressures: relational, informational, moral · Suspense: identity
  • World detail: Two of him disagree in the same voice, so Kaelin labels the mugs.
  • Weather: The heat keeps all three of them in the flat, one of them in the wall.

Chapter 15 — Locked (midpoint)

12 May · Elena · Vila Verde · 6.4 min

The plan is locked. Two thousand four hundred node keepers across Earth, three lunar habitats and Cobalt Basin will cut at the moment of opposition. Elena will anchor through the Spire line. Aur'k will hold her boundary, and Daniel will cut the Spire line last. There's one night of celebration, and Kaelin teaches Vila Verde to bet on Mars's weather.

  • Wants: One night off from the end of the world.
  • Against: Everyone they love is now on one list.
  • Choice and cost: They commit to a single day, and there is no second date.
  • Carries forward (threat): The next morning, Wáng publicly announces the "Open Hand conspiracy" as sabotage. The Hollow knows everything copy-Aur'k knows.
  • Pressures: operational, relational, informational, institutional · Suspense: temporal
  • World detail: The keepers' list is paper, carried house to house, because nothing on the system is safe from the Directorate.
  • Weather: Kaelin teaches Vila Verde to bet on Mars's weather, because Earth's is too grim to bet on.

Act II-B — The offer

Chapter 16 — Reform

20 May · Rachel · Singapore · 5.9 min

Wáng offers Rachel reform. Keep one supervised core "just in case", with Rachel herself running it, and full funding for sleeper care. It would save her job, her city's systems and Joaquín's street. The reform goes to a vote of both blocs on 4 August, opposition day.

  • Wants: A way to save her city without the Directorate.
  • Against: Wáng's offer, which makes sense.
  • Choice and cost: She asks for a week, and gives the Hollow a week of her doubt.
  • Carries forward (debt): Wáng: You built the first one. Who better to keep it safe?
  • Pressures: institutional, moral, relational · Suspense: political
  • World detail: The reform budget funds sleeper care only in linked districts, where the Directorate can monitor the beds.
  • Weather: Wáng's reform budget sits beside a heat budget: cooling for the linked districts first.

Chapter 17 — Heat dome

1 June · Kaelin · the Gulf Crescent and the Mediterranean · 5.2 min

The Hollow fights back the only way it knows, by being needed. It sees a heat dome coming over the Gulf and the Mediterranean and says nothing until the grid fails. The Directorate's system is the only thing keeping cooling centres alive, and Kaelin needs it on every rescue. In the linked cooling centres, people drift by the hundred.

  • Wants: Everyone in the cooling centres alive.
  • Against: The system that keeps them cool is the one taking them.
  • Choice and cost: He uses the system to save lives while it takes others.
  • Carries forward (threat): Node keepers start dropping out. They're too afraid to cut what keeps their grandmothers cool.
  • Pressures: operational, moral, institutional · Suspense: physical
  • World detail: Cooling-centre beds are linked for monitoring, so every bed is a door.
  • Weather: The heat dome over the Gulf and the Mediterranean, and the grid failing under it.

Chapter 18 — Proof of life

8 June · Elena · New York, the Spire line's sealed vault · 4.8 min

Through the Spire line, Soria offers restoration: whole, real, home. Elena tests her with questions only Soria could answer, and every answer is right.

  • Wants: Certainty that it's her.
  • Against: Every answer is right.
  • Choice and cost: Elena starts to believe. Working in New York through the week, she stops telling Daniel, on their calls home, where she goes at night.
  • Carries forward (threat): Soria: Come in alone, and you'll never lose me again.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, informational · Suspense: identity
  • World detail: The vault has one chair, bolted to the floor.
  • Weather: The vault is the coolest room in New York in June, and that is part of why she stays.

Chapter 19 — The booking

15 June · Aur'k · Vila Verde · 4.5 min

Aur'k and Kaelin are slowly rebuilding. Aur'k trains node keepers to hold their boundaries on the day. He sees that Elena is bleeding.

  • Wants: Elena safe on the day.
  • Against: Her privacy, and his own old habit of keeping things to himself.
  • Choice and cost: Aur'k decides not to tell Kaelin first, and to go straight to Daniel. This time he doesn't keep a secret.
  • Carries forward (question): He finds a private session booked at the Spire line for 1 a.m.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, informational · Suspense: moral
  • World detail: Spire line sessions are booked like operating theatres, and the booking shows a single name.
  • Weather: Keepers train in a borrowed cold store, because the cooperative hall is too hot to think in.

Chapter 20 — The threshold

16 June · Elena · the Spire line's vault; Vila Verde · 4.4 min

At 1 a.m., Elena stands at the Spire line alone, as Book 1 Elena would have. She stops. She takes the first suborbital home to Vila Verde, wakes Daniel before dawn and tells him: I want to go in. He doesn't forbid it, and he doesn't leave. They decide together that she goes in only on the day, as the anchor, with witnesses.

  • Wants: Soria back, alone.
  • Against: Daniel asleep at home, and everything she promised him.
  • Choice and cost: She goes home and tells him. Elena gives up the chance of having Soria back alone.
  • Carries forward (debt): Daniel: Then I'm the one who cuts the Spire line.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, operational · Suspense: relational
  • World detail: The vault door logs who leaves as well as who enters, and tonight it logs her leaving.
  • Weather: Before dawn is Vila Verde's only cool hour, and that is when she wakes Daniel.

Chapter 21 — No

25 June · Rachel · the Battery · 4.0 min

Rachel refuses Wáng and tells Joaquín why, and Joaquín leaves her crew for the Directorate. She tells her own crews that on the day, anyone who wants to stay out can.

  • Wants: Joaquín with her.
  • Against: His street, and a better offer.
  • Choice and cost: She refuses Wáng and tells Joaquín the truth. She loses her oldest crew member.
  • Carries forward (threat): The Directorate issues a warrant for Elena.
  • Pressures: relational, institutional, moral · Suspense: political
  • World detail: Her crews' opt-out list is a sheet on the canteen wall, and nobody signs it.
  • Weather: The canteen's opt-out sheet hangs under the heat-alert board.

Chapter 22 — The raid

10 July · Elena · Vila Verde · 3.5 min

Directorate security comes for Elena. Kaelin and Aur'k get her out through Daniel's mesh, and copy-Aur'k sends the search the wrong way. It shouldn't be able to do that against the system it lives in.

  • Wants: Out.
  • Against: Directorate security, who know the plan.
  • Choice and cost: Elena goes underground for the last three weeks, away from Daniel.
  • Carries forward (question): Copy-Aur'k: I don't know if I did that, or if it let me.
  • Pressures: operational, relational, institutional · Suspense: physical
  • World detail: A warrant flags every linked door in the city. The dark towns' doors aren't on the system.
  • Weather: Elena escapes through a dust storm off the dry fields, where the Directorate's drones can't see.

Chapter 23 — Not for me (late revelation)

20 July · Kaelin · the flat · 3.3 min · 15 days

Copy-Aur'k tells Kaelin the truth: he doesn't know whether he is Aur'k, or the Hollow wearing Aur'k, or something new. He asks Kaelin not to decide for him.

  • Wants: To know what the copy is.
  • Against: The copy doesn't know either.
  • Choice and cost: Kaelin, who has spent the trilogy deciding other people's risks, agrees not to decide for him.
  • Carries forward (debt): The copy asks to be at the old Gate core on the day, and Kaelin promises to take him.
  • Pressures: relational, moral, informational · Suspense: identity
  • World detail: The copy has started counting the delay to Mars aloud, the way Kaelin counts under stress.
  • Weather: Night heat that never breaks; the copy counts the delay aloud over the air conditioner.

Act III — Letting go

Chapter 24 — The eve

3 August · Rachel · the Battery · 3.2 min

Most of Rachel's crews stay. The Open Hand has 1,900 of its 2,400 keepers left.

  • Wants: Enough keepers to make it hold.
  • Against: Five hundred gone, and Wáng on the line.
  • Choice and cost: She lets Wáng's last call ring out. Rachel has nothing left to lose but the city.
  • Carries forward (debt): Joaquín sends one line: Don't get yourself killed.
  • Pressures: institutional, relational, moral · Suspense: temporal
  • World detail: On the eve, the Directorate's saves map shows New York at its safest ever.

Chapter 30 — Weather

15 January 2098 · Elena · New York; the roof in Veracruz · 11.3 min

Earth is less safe, and it is free. Elena and Daniel start again as equals. Elena and Rachel meet at a memorial for the Fall's dead, and what they reach is respect, not friendship.
