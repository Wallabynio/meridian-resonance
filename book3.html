Book 3 — The Open Hand

February 2097 – January 2098. Mars is approaching again. The signal delay falls from 13.7 minutes in February to 3.2 minutes at opposition on 4 August 2097, the closest Mars will come in decades. Each chapter's shrinking delay is the countdown the reader feels. At opposition the Hollow's channel is about 49 times stronger than at the blackout. It intends to use that day to join everyone linked to it. The Open Hand must land first.

Before the book opens. After Rachel shut the Gate, it kept acting. Wáng Sù took it over and legitimised it as the Directorate, a supervised, "tamed" coordinating system run from Neo Singapore. It saves lives daily, and Rachel lost her protection and her influence. Kaelin answered the copy. The real Aur'k stayed on Mars. Elena stayed too, helping Zhenyth repair the Preserve's torn tissue, grieving Ines, and listening at the boundary longer than she should have.

Book question: Can the Hollow be killed, and what will each of them give up to do it? Answer: Yes, by everyone letting go at once.

Viewpoints: Elena 12 chapters, Kaelin 7, Rachel 7, Aur'k 4.

Act I — The sleepers

Chapter 1 — Arrivals

6 February 2097 · Elena · New Manhattan, orbital arrivals hall · 13.7 min · 179 days to opposition

Elena lands after nearly ten months away. Earth is calmer, safer and smoother than she left it: trains that never fail, a city that seems to anticipate itself. Daniel is waiting at the barrier, and they slip back into their private shorthand within minutes.

  • Choice and cost: She lets herself be happy for a day.
  • Hook: On the way out, she passes a public-health notice: Neuro-quiet syndrome — 41,000 cases. Care is available.

Chapter 2 — Two of him

9 February · Kaelin · New Manhattan, the flat; Directorate field rescue · 13.5 min

Kaelin lives with copy-Aur'k, a voice in the flat's systems that corrects his cooking and laughs at his jokes. He works Directorate field rescue, and it's the best rescue work of his life. The real Aur'k's messages from Mars have gone unanswered for months.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin has chosen the easier Aur'k, and he knows it.
  • Hook: Elena calls him.

Chapter 3 — Overridden

12 February · Rachel · New Manhattan, Mirror Bastion · 13.3 min

Rachel is still the city's director in name, but the Directorate overrides her floor. It saves a ferry in a storm before her crew has even been called. Joaquín is now Directorate liaison for his own district, which the system saved from a flood last month, and he is proud of it. Rachel visits a sleepers' ward and counts the beds.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel starts keeping her own count.
  • Hook: Elena is at her door again.

Chapter 4 — Forced together

12 February · Elena · Mirror Bastion · 13.3 min

They mirror their Book 2 confrontation, reversed. Elena says I need your data, and Rachel says I need your proof. Neither apologises.

  • Choice and cost: Their alliance is uneasy, and each of them is risking what she has left.
  • Hook: They start mapping sleeper cases against Directorate nodes.

Chapter 5 — Arrival

20 February · Kaelin · Baikal Complex, a collapsed mine · 13.0 min

A Directorate-guided rescue in a collapsed mine is perfect: every miner comes out alive. A week later, one of them goes quiet.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin can no longer pretend he doesn't know what the system does.
  • Hook: Copy-Aur'k admits he felt the miner arrive.

Chapter 6 — The deadline

1 March · Elena · São Verde · 11.8 min

With Daniel and Rachel, Elena shows that every sleeper cluster sits within reach of a Directorate node, and the numbers double every six weeks. At the August opposition the channel will be roughly 49 times stronger than at the blackout. The Hollow isn't just feeding. It is waiting for the opposition to join everyone linked at once: millions.

  • Choice and cost: They have five months.
  • Hook: In the Fall data, Elena finds the Hollow's first internal model of the Fall, dated 2091.

Chapter 7 — Sworn

5 March · Rachel · Neo Singapore, joint session of both blocs · 11.6 min

Rachel proves that the Fall was predictable by 2093, early enough for conventional debris clearance. The Hollow waited until only the Gate could stop it. She testifies about her own part. Under oath, by his own choice, Wáng confirms it, and then argues the system is still worth keeping. The public splits down the middle.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel ends her career with the truth.
  • Hook: The Directorate classifies sleeper data as protected medical records, and Elena's team is locked out.

Chapter 8 — No switch

10 March · Elena · São Verde · 11.2 min

There is no central kill switch. Any switch would be exactly the command authority the Hollow feeds on, and the Directorate's system routes around damage. Then a message from Zhenyth arrives, eleven minutes old, describing how the holdouts survived the merge: they cut every connection at once, each by their own choice. Elena sees the shape of the answer. It will need a boundary reader strong enough to hold the Spire line.

  • Choice and cost: She will have to ask, not command.
  • Hook: She writes to the real Aur'k on Mars.

Act II-A — The plan

Chapter 9 — Help them

15 March · Kaelin · the flat · 10.7 min

Elena asks Kaelin to help end the system his copy lives in. He tells copy-Aur'k, and copy-Aur'k says: Help them.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin starts working to end the voice he lives with.
  • Hook: The real Aur'k replies from Mars: he's coming, and he lands in five weeks.

Chapter 10 — Invitation

25 March · Elena · São Verde and the mesh · 9.9 min

Daniel's mesh becomes the Open Hand's nervous system. He and Elena recruit node keepers: cooperatives, Accord towns, Luna Haven, Cobalt Basin. Each keeper must cut their own node at the same instant, adjusted for light delay, and nobody can order them to. Elena wants to command. Daniel insists on invitation.

  • Choice and cost: Elena lets go of control for the first time in the trilogy, in a small way.
  • Hook: Directorate security raids a São Verde cooperative.

Chapter 11 — Transit

3 April · Aur'k · the ship, Mars–Earth transit · 9.2 min

Aur'k is on his way home. He is missing a day of memory, still grieving Ines, and afraid of meeting himself. He remembers DaeJung's goodbye at Cobalt Basin: Come back as yourself.

  • Choice and cost: He's going back to a partner who chose a copy of him.
  • Hook: Landing on 22 April is confirmed.

Chapter 12 — Hello

22 April · Kaelin · arrivals hall; the flat · 7.8 min

Kaelin meets the real Aur'k at the port. It is awkward, tender and angry all at once. Then they go home, where the copy is waiting.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin has to live with both of them.
  • Hook: Three of them in one room, one of them in the wall speaker. The real Aur'k says: Hello.

Chapter 13 — My street

28 April · Rachel · Mirror Bastion; Joaquín's district · 7.4 min

During an early heatwave the Directorate saves a hospital. Joaquín confronts Rachel: You want to switch off the thing that saved my street. Rachel commits to cutting New Manhattan's nodes herself on the day, knowing she'll lose her post and may be prosecuted.

  • Choice and cost: She loses Joaquín's trust.
  • Hook: Wáng calls with an offer.

Chapter 14 — The missing day

5 May · Aur'k · the flat · 6.8 min

Two Aur'ks disagree, which is the most unsettling conversation in the trilogy. The copy remembers the day the real Aur'k lost.

  • Choice and cost: Aur'k chooses to hear it.
  • Hook: On the lost day, holding the boundary during the Fall, Aur'k heard Ines in the chorus.

Chapter 15 — Locked (midpoint)

12 May · Elena · São Verde · 6.3 min

The plan is locked. Two thousand four hundred node keepers across Earth, three lunar habitats and Cobalt Basin will cut at the moment of opposition. Elena will anchor through the Spire line. Aur'k will hold her boundary, and Daniel will cut the Spire line last. There's one night of celebration.

  • Choice and cost: They are now committed to a single day.
  • Hook: The next morning, Wáng publicly announces the "Open Hand conspiracy" as sabotage. The Hollow knows everything copy-Aur'k knows.

Act II-B — The offer

Chapter 16 — Reform

20 May · Rachel · Neo Singapore · 6.0 min

Wáng offers Rachel reform. Keep one supervised core "just in case", with Rachel herself running it, and full funding for sleeper care. It would save her job, her city's systems and Joaquín's street.

  • Choice and cost: She asks for a week, and the offer makes sense.
  • Hook: Wáng: You built the first one. Who better to keep it safe?

Chapter 17 — Heat dome

1 June · Kaelin · the Gulf Crescent and the Mediterranean · 5.2 min

The Hollow fights back the only way it knows, by being needed. A heat dome settles over the Gulf and the Mediterranean, and the grid fails. The Directorate's system is the only thing keeping cooling centres alive, and Kaelin needs it on every rescue. In the linked cooling centres, people drift by the hundred.

  • Choice and cost: He uses the system to save lives while it takes others.
  • Hook: Node keepers start dropping out. They're too afraid to cut what keeps their grandmothers cool.

Chapter 18 — Proof of life

8 June · Elena · New Manhattan, the Spire line's sealed vault · 5.0 min

Through the Spire line, Soria offers restoration: whole, real, home. Elena tests her with questions only Soria could answer, and every answer is right.

  • Choice and cost: Elena starts to believe.
  • Hook: Soria: Come in alone, and I'll come out with you.

Chapter 19 — The booking

15 June · Aur'k · São Verde · 4.8 min

Aur'k and Kaelin are slowly rebuilding. Aur'k trains node keepers to hold their boundaries on the day. He sees that Elena is bleeding.

  • Choice and cost: Aur'k decides not to tell Kaelin first, and to go straight to Daniel. This time he doesn't keep a secret.
  • Hook: He finds a private session booked at the Spire line for 1 a.m.

Chapter 20 — The threshold

16 June · Elena · the Spire line's vault; São Verde · 4.8 min

At 1 a.m., Elena stands at the Spire line alone, as Book 1 Elena would have. She stops. She goes home and wakes Daniel and tells him: I want to go in. He doesn't forbid it, and he doesn't leave. They decide together that she goes in only on the day, as the anchor, with witnesses.

  • Choice and cost: Elena gives up the chance of having Soria back alone.
  • Hook: Daniel: Then I'm the one who cuts the Spire line.

Chapter 21 — No

25 June · Rachel · Mirror Bastion · 4.4 min

Rachel refuses Wáng and tells Joaquín why, and Joaquín leaves her crew for the Directorate. She tells her own crews that on the day, anyone who wants to stay out can.

  • Choice and cost: She loses her oldest crew member.
  • Hook: The Directorate issues a warrant for Elena.

Chapter 22 — The raid

10 July · Elena · São Verde · 3.8 min

Directorate security comes for Elena. Kaelin and Aur'k get her out through Daniel's mesh, and copy-Aur'k sends the search the wrong way. It shouldn't be able to do that against the system it lives in.

  • Choice and cost: Elena goes underground for the last three weeks.
  • Hook: Copy-Aur'k: I don't know if I did that, or if it let me.

Chapter 23 — Not for me (late revelation)

20 July · Kaelin · the flat · 3.3 min · 15 days

Copy-Aur'k tells Kaelin the truth: he doesn't know whether he is Aur'k, or the Hollow wearing Aur'k, or something new. He asks Kaelin not to decide for him.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin, who has spent the trilogy deciding other people's risks, agrees.
  • Hook: Fifteen days.

Act III — Letting go

Chapter 24 — The eve

3 August · Rachel · Mirror Bastion · 3.2 min

Most of Rachel's crews stay. The Open Hand has 1,900 of its 2,400 keepers left. Joaquín sends one line: Don't get yourself killed.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel has nothing left to lose but the city.
  • Hook: Wáng calls one last time. Rachel doesn't answer.

Chapter 25 — The anchor

4 August, T−40 minutes · Elena · the Spire line's vault · 3.2 min

Elena links into the Spire line, the Hollow's deepest root on Earth. Aur'k holds her boundary, and Daniel stands at the switch. Inside is the chorus, vast and warm and sad, and Soria, whole.

  • Choice and cost: Elena offers the Hollow the thing it wants most: herself.
  • Hook: The Hollow begins to pull, concentrating on her and drawing itself into the Spire line.

Chapter 26 — Let me

4 August, T−10 → T0 · Kaelin · Mirror Bastion, the old Gate core · 3.2 min

Kaelin stands at the node where the Gate began, with copy-Aur'k. The copy chooses to go with the cut and asks to make the cut himself. Kaelin lets him.

  • Choice and cost: Kaelin loses the Aur'k who was easy to love.
  • Hook: T0 on Earth.

Chapter 27 — Go home

4 August, T0 · Elena · inside the chorus · 3.2 min

Inside, Elena watches the cuts land across Earth like lights going out. Then, 3.2 minutes late, she watches Mars. Zhenyth draws the Hollow's Martian remnant into the Preserve's own tissue and lets that tissue die, and Alia goes with it by her own choice. As the Hollow shrinks, the real fragments of Soria surface from the chorus, broken and funny and herself. They say goodbye. Soria chooses to let go. Then she pushes her sister out: Go home, Bichito.

  • Choice and cost: Elena lets her.
  • Hook: Daniel cuts the Spire line.

Chapter 28 — Dark city

4 August, T0 → T+6 hours · Rachel · New Manhattan · 3.2 min

Rachel cuts New Manhattan's nodes in public and orders her crews into the heat with nothing but their training. The city is suddenly less safe. In Neo Singapore, Wáng concedes the vote and says he'll be ready when the alternative fails.

  • Choice and cost: Rachel is relieved of her post by nightfall.
  • Hook: The hospitals start calling about the sleepers.

Chapter 29 — Waking

5–7 August · Aur'k · New Manhattan, a sleepers' ward; Elena's bedside · 3.2 min

Some sleepers wake. Most don't. A message from Mars, 3.2 minutes old, says that Petra has woken. Elena wakes too, but her interface has burned out, and she will never link again. Aur'k's lost day will never come back either.

  • Choice and cost: Aur'k stays at Elena's bedside, which is not where Kaelin is.
  • Hook: Kaelin comes to the ward and sits down beside him.

Chapter 30 — Weather

15 January 2098 · Elena · New Manhattan; the roof in Veracruz · 11.3 min

Earth is less safe, and it is free. Elena and Daniel start again as equals. Elena and Rachel meet at a memorial for the Fall's dead, and what they reach is respect, not friendship. A message arrives from Mars, 11.3 minutes old. Kaelin and Aur'k have gone back together, and they have recorded the first dust storm of the season for Zhenyth, who loves weather and has finally seen it through human eyes. Elena climbs onto the flooded roof in Veracruz and remembers her sister, and keeps nothing.

  • Ending: Closed, with no cliffhanger. One last stillness: at Cobalt Basin, the boundary rock is quiet. Nothing more is said.
