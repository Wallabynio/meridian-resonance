Profile

Want, wound, line and arc

Who she wasElena's older sister, born 2050, died 2089 aged 39. A hospice nurse who volunteered for the lattice programme because she believed the link could let the dying be understood
What speaksThe Hollow's mask built from her, wrapped around real surviving fragments of her. Neither Elena nor the reader can be sure which is talking until Book 3
ParticularsBossy older-sister energy, irreverent, gallows humour learned at bedsides. Hates being comforted. Calls Elena Bichito ("little bug"), a name no record holds
Strength → dangerRefuses to be comforted → refuses to be rescued, which is how she finally lets go
TellThe real Soria contradicts what the Hollow wants. When "Soria" pushes Elena toward the bridge, the gate or restoration, it's the mask. When she teases, refuses, or tells Elena to go home, it's her
WantTo be herself, not a tool, a proof or someone else's grief
VoiceWarm, teasing, interrupts. Her sentences stop where memory runs out
BookHow she appearsWhat she forces
1Fragments in the warnings; one real conversation (Ch 15); a last fragment after the cutElena's secrecy and the break with Daniel; the cut
2The offer: "they kept enough of me to bring me home"Elena's journey to Mars
3Whole, offered back; then the real Soria inside the chorusElena's goodbye. Soria chooses to let go
