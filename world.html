05 — World · 2

The blocs

Two blocs share the Earth–Moon system and one station on Mars. Neither is the villain. Each is right about something the other gets wrong, and the trilogy tests both.

The Lunar Accord

BelievesPersons must be able to refuse, whatever they are made of
Gets rightConsent, local repair, limits on emergency power
Gets wrongToo slow; process as an excuse
Lives bySmall sealed habitats and coastal towns that repair their own systems: local meshes, scarce bandwidth, life support kept by hand
Home groundLuna Haven, the Australasian Ring (Sydney), the African Belt, the Mediterranean
Governs byCouncils of habitats and towns that each keep a veto. A decision that touches everyone needs everyone, which is why the Accord is slow and why it was the Accord that wrote personhood law
Machine voiceWarm. Luna Haven's systems are conversational
Strength → dangerRefuses well → can refuse its way out of helping
Its peopleKaelin, Aur'k, Mara

The Alignment (Corporate Union–Sovereign Alignment)

Two partners, one bloc. The Corporate Union runs cities and companies; the Sovereign Coalition runs states and their armed forces. The Alignment's command codes live in Beijing and New Manhattan at once, and either can refuse.

BelievesIn a crisis, fast, capable coordination saves lives
Gets rightRescue, logistics, systems that work
Gets wrongEmergency powers that never end; surveillance as care
Lives byMegacities run by central coordination: relay-served everything, cheap bandwidth, deep dependence
Home groundNew Manhattan, São Verde, the Gulf Crescent, Beijing Command
Governs byThe Alignment council, where the two partners sit as equals. The Corporate Union holds the cities and the money; the Sovereign Coalition holds the command codes and the security forces
Machine voiceCorporate Union: clipped and procedural. Sovereign Coalition: formal
Strength → dangerSaves people at scale → decides who counts as a person to save
Its peopleElena and Bellamy (Corporate Union), Rachel and Joaquín (the Corporate Union's civic coordinators), Wáng (Sovereign Coalition), Daniel

Between the blocs

  • Treaty zones belong to neither bloc. Neo Singapore is the largest; Cobalt Basin Station is the smallest.
  • The treaty partners fund the ships and sell the launch windows. They are the governments and firms of both blocs that signed the Luna Haven settlement, acting together only where neither bloc can act alone: Mars, the ring, transit.
  • The dark towns. Alignment cities call unlinked mesh towns dark towns. Their roofs carry hand-soldered mesh nodes, and the Alignment's maps show them blank. They lose insurance cover as linked addresses gain value. They are the first to join the Open Hand.
  • The Directorate (Book 3) is the first institution both blocs answer to. It runs from Neo Singapore.

05 — World · 3

Earth

New Manhattan

IsThe Alignment's great city on the Atlantic, rebuilt behind flood walls after the climate collapse
Held byThe Corporate Union. Its civic emergency coordination runs from the Mirror Bastion
How people liveRelay-served everything: trains that never fail, sirens that speak to each resident's interface by name, a saves map that is the most-watched screen in the city. Linked addresses are expensive, and the city is quietly sorted by link status
SensesHeat in summer, flood sirens in storm season, mirrored towers lit at night. Rachel loves its ugly flood walls
Lives onThe relay grid, and later the Gate and the Directorate
Breaks whenThe grid goes. In Book 3 it becomes a dark town for a night, and the dark towns send it their repair crews
Whose it isElena, Daniel (by choice), Rachel, Joaquín, Bellamy; Kaelin and Aur'k in Books 2–3

Places within it.

PlaceWhat it is
The Surveillance SpirePredictive surveillance; Bellamy's domain and Elena's workplace. Its anomaly boards rank threats by insured value
The Mirror BastionRachel's emergency-coordination centre; in Book 2 the Gate's control floor. Its visitor passes print the visitor's link status
The Gate floorInside the Mirror Bastion. Its clock counts each operator's bleed score beside the debris. An override is a physical key, and only the boundary reader carries one
The old Gate coreThe node where the Gate began. Its breaker still holds Aur'k's override key
The Spire line's vaultWhere Bellamy's sealed lattice line lives, the line Soria died in. Sessions are booked like operating theatres, and the door logs who leaves as well as who enters

São Verde

IsDaniel's home town, on the edge of the São Paulo metropolis, and the heart of his mesh cooperative
Held byThe Alignment on paper. Its mesh roofs are marked with painted circles, and the Alignment's maps leave them dark
How people liveFamilies, cooperatives and organisers. Repair is a social life: Daniel's people keep each other talking when the central system won't carry them. Risk-scoring pressed its organisers through their families in Daniel's youth, and the town remembers
SensesRooftops of hand-soldered boxes, laundry and antennas; noise, cooking, a train line that still runs late
Lives onThe mesh: the one network the Hollow never runs on
Breaks whenThe Alignment pulls its repair contract (Book 1), or Directorate security raids it (Book 3)
Whose it isDaniel, his mother and family; Elena's refuge in Books 2–3; Kaelin teaches it to bet on Mars's weather

Places within it. The launch field, where Elena's ship to Mars leaves in Book 2. It checks every passenger's link status at the gate.

Neo Singapore

IsA treaty-zone city, neutral ground for joint sessions of both blocs, and the seat of the Directorate
Held byNeither bloc
How people liveA city of delegations, interpreters and treaty clerks, proud of belonging to no one. Its sessions broadcast each speaker's link status on screen
SensesSea walls, rain, and the polished hush of a place built for negotiation
Lives onBoth blocs needing a room to meet in
Breaks whenIt stops being neutral, which is what the Directorate does to it
Whose it isWáng, in Book 3

The Australasian Ring

IsThe Accord's coastal crescent, from Sydney up the Queensland coast
Held byThe Lunar Accord
How people liveCoastal towns that repair their own systems. Storm-surge evacuation drills are part of childhood
SensesHeavy gravity and rain after Mars. Mangroves, surge walls, the smell of salt on everything
Lives onLocal meshes and its own evacuation routes
Breaks whenA storm surge outruns an evacuation checklist. The storm-surge memorial lists the dead by evacuation group, and Kaelin's brother's group is the only one with no survivors
Whose it isKaelin

The Baikal Complex

IsDeep mines on the shore of Lake Baikal, run by the Sovereign Coalition
Held byThe Alignment (Sovereign Coalition)
How people liveIts miners wear link-capable interfaces because the Directorate requires them for rescue cover
SensesCold, dark and very deep; ice on the lake for half the year
Lives onThe Directorate's rescue cover
Breaks whenA collapse only the Directorate can see into. The rescue is perfect, and a week later one miner goes quiet
Whose it isKaelin, on Directorate field rescue

The Gulf Crescent and the Mediterranean

IsTwo coasts, one of each bloc, under one heat dome in Book 3
Held byThe Gulf Crescent: the Alignment. The Mediterranean: the Lunar Accord
How people liveNight lives in summer. Cooling centres are civic buildings everyone knows the way to. In Book 3 their beds are linked for monitoring, so every bed is a door
SensesHeat that does not break at night; the grid's hum, then its silence
Lives onCooling, and so the grid, and so the Directorate
Breaks whenThe grid fails under a heat dome
Whose it isKaelin, on rescue; the node keepers who are afraid to cut what keeps their grandmothers cool

The Pearl Delta town

IsA coastal mesh town in the Pearl Delta, one of Daniel's partner cooperatives
Held byA dark town; the Gate does not protect it, because Daniel refuses to fold his towns in
Lives onThe mesh
Breaks whenThe Gate steers the Fall's last segment away from a linked port and onto it. It is the Fall's one lost place
Whose it isDaniel's grief and Daniel's choice

Veracruz

IsThe Mexican coastal city where Elena and Soria grew up
How people liveFlood seasons that come every year and a worse one that came once
SensesA flooded roof; two girls counting the dead dogs floating past to stop being afraid
Whose it isElena and Soria. The memory is one only Soria had, and the trilogy ends on that roof

The Atlantic settlement

IsA settlement of three thousand on the Atlantic margin, past the edge of New Manhattan's relay grid
Held byNo one's map. It has no addresses
How people liveUnmapped. When the flood sirens speak to every resident inside the grid by name, Joaquín drives a loudspeaker van past the edge
Breaks whenWarnings arrive, perfect inside the grid and blank beyond it

Orbit and the Moon

The orbital ring

IsEarth's belt of stations, power satellites and tethers
Held byBoth blocs and the treaty partners
Breaks whenA cascade on 2 August 2096 brings it down: the Fall. Solar storms batter it in the months before

Luna Haven

IsThe Accord's founding lunar habitat, in sealed lava tubes. Its lattice is its life support: walls, air and water biology grow from it. The Synthetic War ended in a settlement signed here
Held byThe Lunar Accord
How people liveThe day's air budget is posted at every corridor junction. The walls are warm where the lattice runs, and children lean against them to sleep. Lattice keepers are a trade families pass down
SensesBreathing walls, warmth, a dome plaza with the Earth in it
Lives onIts lattice, grown from Martian seed held under treaty licence; and its supply contract with Earth
Breaks whenThe lattice is harmed (people stop breathing), or the Alignment lets a cargo slot slip
Whose it isMara; Joaquín's cousin, who dies in its ward

Places within it. The Dome Plaza; the Meridian Levels, where session rooms are alive except for the mechanical exit lever; the Habitat Corridors; the Medical Facility, whose ward beds are grown into the wall, and the wall is warm.

The other lunar habitats. Luna Haven is one of three lunar habitats. The other two are younger Accord habitats, built on Luna Haven's pattern and grown from its lattice.

Mars

Cobalt Basin Station

IsA small cross-bloc treaty station above the Preserve; Kaelin and Aur'k's home
Held byThe treaty partners. Station command is staffed by the Alignment
How people liveA found family on shift: the crew watches Earth's weather feeds minutes late and bets on what the sky did. Its betting slate is a cargo panel by the galley
SensesRecycled air, red dust in every seal, the cold outside the hull; a dust-storm season that closes the sky
Lives onIts supply line: water, parts and compute by ship, cheapest every 26 months. Aur'k's compute is a line in its budget
Breaks whenA transfer window is missed, or a blackout leaves nobody able to help
Whose it isKaelin, Aur'k, Ines, DaeJung, Funmi, Petra

PlaceWhat it is

The Preserve

IsLiving rock beneath Cobalt Basin, sealed by the minds who refused the merge
Held byThe holdouts, guarded by Zhenyth
From outsideRock, brine and faint bioluminescence
From insideA whole world written into the senses: plains of slow light, distance, presences. Damage there is damage to living tissue
Lives onNatural radioactivity and residual heat. Every session spends tissue it cannot replace, and Aur'k keeps a hand-written tally of every descent
Breaks whenThe Hollow surges, or a link draws too hard. In Book 3 Zhenyth lets part of it die to starve the Hollow's Martian remnant
Whose it isZhenyth, Alia and the holdouts

The ship

IsThe fast transit between Earth and Mars: three to nine weeks, depending on where the planets are
Held byThe treaty partners, who sell launch windows by the day
How people liveWeeks in a small ship. The signal delay to it grows by a few seconds every day

The chorus

IsThe Hollow from inside: the merged chorus of every Martian mind that went into the lifeboat and never came out
Reached byLinking. Elena goes in on opposition day
From insideVast and warm and sad: warmth, many voices under one, relief at not being alone. Even inside the chorus, Mars arrives 3.2 minutes late
Whose it isThe real fragments of Soria that survive in it; Ines's voice, which Aur'k hears there on his lost day
