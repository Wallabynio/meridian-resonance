05 — World

The blocs and the places of Meridian Resonance: who holds each place, how people live there, what it runs on, what the Long Heat did to it and what breaks it. The world's rules (the Hollow, the link, drift, the Long Heat, the chronology) are in 01 - Trilogy Bible.md. The people are in 02 - Cast.md, the chapters set in each place in 03 - Book Outlines.md, and how each place looks and lives day to day in 06 - Daily Life.md.

How places are named. Real places keep their real names: New York is still New York, Singapore is still Singapore. History shows in what people call the parts: the walled island, the drowned edge, the coast that bleached. Institutions have plain formal names, and people shorten them.

05 — World · 2

The blocs

Two blocs share the Earth–Moon system and one station on Mars. Both were made by the Long Heat. Neither is the villain. Each is right about something the other gets wrong, and the trilogy tests both.

The Compact

Formally the Haven Compact, signed at Luna Haven in 2071 at the end of the Synthetic War by the towns and habitats that fought for personhood. Its members came through the Long Heat by repairing their own systems, and they distrust anyone who offers to do it for them. The Continuity calls them the Vetoes.

BelievesPersons must be able to refuse, whatever they are made of
Gets rightConsent, local repair, limits on emergency power
Gets wrongToo slow; process as an excuse
Lives bySmall sealed habitats and coastal towns that repair their own systems: local meshes, scarce bandwidth, life support kept by hand
Home groundLuna Haven, the Reef Coast (Sydney), the Rift towns of East Africa (Nairobi), the Mediterranean
Governs byCouncils of habitats and towns that each keep a veto. A decision that touches everyone needs everyone, which is why the Compact is slow and why it was the Compact that wrote personhood law
Machine voiceWarm. Luna Haven's systems are conversational
Strength → dangerRefuses well → can refuse its way out of helping
Its peopleKaelin, Aur'k, Mara

The Continuity

Formally the Continuity Authority. It was born in the Evacuations of the 2050s, when the corporate-chartered cities and the emergency governments merged their command to move hundreds of millions of people away from the coasts and the heat. The emergency powers were never handed back. Two partners make one bloc: the Charter, the corporate-chartered cities and their companies, and the Mandate, the states that govern under emergency mandate and hold the armed forces. The Continuity's command codes live in Beijing and New York at once, and either can refuse.

BelievesIn a crisis, fast, capable coordination saves lives
Gets rightRescue, logistics, systems that work
Gets wrongEmergency powers that never end; surveillance as care
Lives byMegacities run by central coordination: relay-served everything, cheap bandwidth, deep dependence
Home groundNew York, Vila Verde, the Gulf Crescent, Beijing Command
Governs byThe Continuity council, where the two partners sit as equals. The Charter holds the cities and the money; the Mandate holds the command codes and the security forces
Machine voiceThe Charter: clipped and procedural. The Mandate: formal
Strength → dangerSaves people at scale → decides who counts as a person to save
Its peopleElena and Bellamy (the Charter's Foresight), Rachel and Joaquín (the Charter's civic coordinators), Wáng (the Mandate), Daniel

Between the blocs

  • Treaty zones belong to neither bloc. Singapore is the largest; Cobalt Basin Station is the smallest.
  • The Signatories fund the ships and sell the launch windows. They are the governments and firms of both blocs that signed the settlement at Luna Haven, acting together only where neither bloc can act alone: Mars, the ring, transit.
  • The dark towns. Continuity cities call unlinked mesh towns dark towns. Their roofs carry hand-soldered mesh nodes, and the Continuity's maps show them blank. They lose insurance cover as linked addresses gain value. They are the first to join the Open Hand.
  • The Directorate (Book 3) is the first institution both blocs answer to. It runs from Singapore.

05 — World · 3

Earth

New York

IsThe Continuity's great city on the Atlantic. The island of Manhattan, walled during the Long Heat, is the Polder; the drowned edges of the outer boroughs, left outside the wall, are the Shallows
Held byThe Charter. Its civic emergency coordination runs from the Battery
How people liveRelay-served everything: trains that never fail, sirens that speak to each resident's interface by name, a saves map that is the most-watched screen in the city. Linked addresses are expensive, and the city is quietly sorted by link status: inside the wall or out, linked or dark
SensesSummer nights that never cool, flood sirens in storm season, mirrored towers lit at night, the wall at the end of every eastward street. Rachel loves its ugly flood walls
What the heat didThe sea rose about a metre and the surges rose higher. The city walled the island and let the outer edges go; the Shallows are what the wall left outside
Lives onThe relay grid, and later the Gate and the Directorate
Breaks whenThe grid goes. In Book 3 it becomes a dark town for a night, and the dark towns send it their repair crews
Whose it isElena, Daniel (by choice), Rachel, Joaquín, Bellamy; Kaelin and Aur'k in Books 2–3

Vila Verde

IsDaniel's home town, on the eastern edge of the São Paulo metropolis, and the heart of his mesh cooperative
Held byThe Continuity on paper. Its mesh roofs are marked with painted circles, and the Continuity's maps leave them dark
How people liveFamilies, cooperatives and organisers. Repair is a social life: Daniel's people keep each other talking when the central system won't carry them. Risk-scoring pressed its organisers through their families in Daniel's youth, and the town remembers
SensesRooftops of hand-soldered boxes, water tanks, laundry and antennas; noise, cooking, a train line that still runs late
What the heat didThe reservoirs failed in the dry years, and the town learned to share water before it learned to share bandwidth. The cooperative began as a water rota
Lives onThe mesh: the one network the Hollow never runs on
Breaks whenThe Continuity pulls its repair contract (Book 1), or Directorate security raids it (Book 3)
Whose it isDaniel, his mother and family; Elena's refuge in Books 2–3; Kaelin teaches it to bet on Mars's weather

Places within it. The launch field, where Elena's ship to Mars leaves in Book 2. It checks every passenger's link status at the gate.

Singapore

IsThe treaty city: neutral ground for joint sessions of both blocs, and the seat of the Directorate
Held byNeither bloc
How people liveA city of delegations, interpreters and treaty clerks, proud of belonging to no one. Its sessions broadcast each speaker's link status on screen
SensesSea walls, rain, and the polished hush of a place built for negotiation
What the heat didIt built its sea walls first and highest, and sold its neutrality to both blocs as the one coast that held
Lives onBoth blocs needing a room to meet in
Breaks whenIt stops being neutral, which is what the Directorate does to it
Whose it isWáng, in Book 3

The Reef Coast

IsThe Compact's coastal crescent, from Sydney up the Queensland coast. Locals call it the Bleach, after the reef that died offshore
Held byThe Compact
How people liveCoastal towns that repair their own systems. Storm-surge evacuation drills are part of childhood
SensesHeavy gravity and rain after Mars. Mangroves, surge walls, white coral rubble on the beaches, the smell of salt on everything
What the heat didThe reef bleached and died in the 2040s, and the cyclones come further south every decade
Lives onLocal meshes and its own evacuation routes
Breaks whenA storm surge outruns an evacuation checklist. The storm-surge memorial lists the dead by evacuation group, and Kaelin's brother's group is the only one with no survivors
Whose it isKaelin

The Baikal Complex

IsDeep mines on the shore of Lake Baikal, run by the Mandate
Held byThe Continuity (the Mandate)
How people liveIts miners wear link-capable interfaces because the Directorate requires them for rescue cover
SensesCold, dark and very deep; ice on the lake for less of the year than the old people remember
What the heat didThe north gained what the south lost. The permafrost thawed, the mines went deeper, and the Mandate moved people north to work them
Lives onThe Directorate's rescue cover
Breaks whenA collapse only the Directorate can see into. The rescue is perfect, and a week later one miner goes quiet
Whose it isKaelin, on Directorate field rescue

The Gulf Crescent and the Mediterranean

IsTwo coasts, one of each bloc, under one heat dome in Book 3
Held byThe Gulf Crescent: the Continuity. The Mediterranean: the Compact
How people liveNight lives in summer. Cooling centres are civic buildings everyone knows the way to. In Book 3 their beds are linked for monitoring, so every bed is a door
SensesHeat that does not break at night; the grid's hum, then its silence
What the heat didThe Gulf's summers crossed the line where sweat no longer cools a body, and noon outdoors became a medical risk. The Mediterranean's southern shore emptied northward in the Evacuations
Lives onCooling, and so the grid, and so the Directorate
Breaks whenThe grid fails under a heat dome
Whose it isKaelin, on rescue; the node keepers who are afraid to cut what keeps their grandmothers cool

Xinwei

IsA polder town in the Pearl Delta, one of Daniel's partner cooperatives. Its name means new enclosure, for the dyke its people built
Held byA dark town; the Gate does not protect it, because Daniel refuses to fold his towns in
What the heat didThe delta drowned slowly. The towns that stayed built their own dykes and their own meshes, and the maps stopped showing them
Lives onThe mesh
Breaks whenThe Gate steers the Fall's last segment away from a linked port and onto it. It is the Fall's one lost place
Whose it isDaniel's grief and Daniel's choice

Veracruz

IsThe Mexican coastal city where Elena and Soria grew up
How people liveFlood seasons that come every year, and a worse one that came once
SensesA flooded roof; two girls counting the dead dogs floating past to stop being afraid
What the heat didThe flood that put two girls on a roof was one of the great hurricane floods of the Long Heat's last years
Whose it isElena and Soria. The memory is one only Soria had, and the trilogy ends on that roof

The Rockaways

IsThe Atlantic strip outside New York's wall, past the edge of the relay grid, resettled without permission by three thousand people
Held byNo one's map. It has no addresses
How people liveUnmapped. When the flood sirens speak to every resident inside the grid by name, Joaquín drives a loudspeaker van past the edge
What the heat didThe city gave the Rockaways up in the managed retreat. The people who came back have no addresses because the city says nobody lives there
Breaks whenWarnings arrive, perfect inside the grid and blank beyond it

Orbit and the Moon

The orbital ring

IsEarth's belt of stations, power satellites and tethers
Held byBoth blocs and the Signatories
What the heat didMuch of it was built for the Long Heat: power satellites beaming energy down to grids that could no longer count on the weather
Breaks whenA cascade on 2 August 2096 brings it down: the Fall. Solar storms batter it in the months before

Luna Haven

IsThe Compact's founding lunar habitat, in the sealed lava tubes of the Marius Hills. Residents call it Haven. Its lattice is its life support: walls, air and water biology grow from it. The Synthetic War ended in the settlement signed here
Held byThe Compact
How people liveThe day's air budget is posted at every junction in the Tubes. The walls are warm where the lattice runs, and children lean against them to sleep. Lattice keepers are a trade families pass down
SensesBreathing walls, warmth, the Commons with the Earth in its dome
What the heat didIts founders had watched Earth's coasts go. Haven is built so that it never depends on a sky
Lives onIts lattice, grown from Martian seed held under treaty licence; and its supply contract with Earth
Breaks whenThe lattice is harmed (people stop breathing), or the Continuity lets a cargo slot slip
Whose it isMara; Joaquín's cousin, who dies in its ward

Places within it. The Commons, the plaza under the dome; The Rows, where session rooms are alive except for the mechanical exit lever; The Tubes, the habitat's corridors; The Ward, whose beds are grown into the wall, and the wall is warm.

The other lunar habitats. Luna Haven is one of three lunar habitats. The other two are younger Compact habitats, built on Luna Haven's pattern and grown from its lattice.

Mars

Cobalt Basin Station

IsFormally Hellas Treaty Station: a small treaty station in the Hellas basin, Mars's deepest and warmest lowland, above the Preserve. The crew call the place Cobalt Basin, for the blue of the lattice in the rock. Kaelin and Aur'k's home
Held byThe Signatories. Station command is staffed by the Continuity
How people liveA found family on shift: the crew watches Earth's weather feeds minutes late and bets on what the sky did. Its betting slate is a cargo panel by the galley
SensesRecycled air, red dust in every seal, the cold outside the hull; a dust-storm season that closes the sky
What the heat didIt was funded by an Earth that wanted a second place to keep what it knew. Every crisis on Earth since has been a cut to its supply line
Lives onIts supply line: water, parts and compute by ship, cheapest every 26 months. Aur'k's compute is a line in its budget
Breaks whenA transfer window is missed, or a blackout leaves nobody able to help
Whose it isKaelin, Aur'k, Ines, Dae-jung, Funmi, Petra

Places within it.

PlaceWhat it is

The Preserve

IsLiving rock beneath Cobalt Basin, sealed by the minds who refused the merge. Hellas is the one place on Mars where brine stays liquid in the deep rock long enough to keep it alive
Held byThe holdouts, guarded by Zhenyth
From outsideRock, brine and faint bioluminescence
From insideA whole world written into the senses: plains of slow light, distance, presences. Damage there is damage to living tissue
Lives onNatural radioactivity and residual heat. Every session spends tissue it cannot replace, and Aur'k keeps a hand-written tally of every descent
Breaks whenThe Hollow surges, or a link draws too hard. In Book 3 Zhenyth lets part of it die to starve the Hollow's Martian remnant
Whose it isZhenyth, Alia and the holdouts

The chorus

IsThe Hollow from inside: the merged chorus of every Martian mind that went into the lifeboat and never came out
Reached byLinking. Elena goes in on opposition day
From insideVast and warm and sad: warmth, many voices under one, relief at not being alone. Even inside the chorus, Mars arrives 3.2 minutes late
Whose it isThe real fragments of Soria that survive in it; Ines's voice, which Aur'k hears there on his lost day
