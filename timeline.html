Timeline & Pacing

Two views of the same trilogy. The timeline is a dependency-driven chronology — earlier events are included not only for date reference but because they establish the causal conditions for later chapter outcomes — from the 2040s Climate Wars to the Chapter 90 close. The pacing notes are editorial: what improves clarity, causality, and chapter-to-chapter payoff. Neither is a second canon source — where an entry or note touches a rule or mechanic, Laws & Codex remains authoritative.
