World · Earth · The Continuity (the Charter)

New York

The Continuity's great city on the Atlantic: the walled island of the Polder, and the drowned edges of the Shallows

Set in 11 chapters Book 1 · Ch 1–22

What it is

New York

Held by
The Charter. Its civic emergency coordination runs from the Battery
How people live
Relay-served everything: trains that never fail, sirens that speak to each resident's interface by name, a saves map that is the most-watched screen in the city. Linked addresses are expensive, and the city is quietly sorted by link status: inside the wall or out, linked or dark
Senses
Summer nights that never cool, flood sirens in storm season, mirrored towers lit at night, the wall at the end of every eastward street. Rachel loves its ugly flood walls
Lives on
The relay grid, and later the Gate and the Directorate
Breaks when
The grid goes. In Book 3 it becomes a dark town for a night, and the dark towns send it their repair crews

Where the chapters go

  • Joaquín's districtCh 12

    A rebuilt district in the Shallows that the Directorate saved from a flood. His street has a plaque thanking it

    • Mariners' HouseCh 12

      A residence for retired harbour and pump crews in Joaquín's raised-fill district of the Shallows, two streets from his own; six floors, a hundred and forty beds; its lifts shed at noon and its cooling failed top-down on reserve in the 9 May dome; cleared floor by floor on Rachel's name

  • The BatteryCh 4, 12, 16, 21

    Rachel's emergency-coordination centre at Battery Park, built into the sea wall at the island's tip; in Book 2 the Gate's control floor. Its visitor passes print the visitor's link status

  • The PolderCh 3, 4

    The walled island of Manhattan, inside New York's wall; Elena and Daniel's flat, the Spire and the Walk are inside it

    • The WalkCh 3, 4

      The park along the top of the Polder's wall, above the flood gate and the harbour; pump houses hum under it

  • The ShallowsCh 4

    The drowned edges of the outer boroughs, left outside New York's wall; Broad Channel and Joaquín's raised-fill district are in it

    • Broad ChannelCh 4

      A stilt district of the Shallows in the middle of Jamaica Bay; on the grid; 1,412 residents, cleared by siren and flooded at 18:55 on 6 February

  • The SpireCh 1, 6, 22 · the steps

    Foresight's tower: the Charter's predictive service, Bellamy's domain and Elena's workplace. Its anomaly boards rank threats by insured value

  • The Spire line's vaultCh 22 · the door

    Where Bellamy's sealed lattice line lives, the line Soria died in. Sessions are booked like operating theatres, and the door logs who leaves as well as who enters

  • The flatCh 3, 8, 13, 18 · kitchen, roof

    Kaelin and Aur'k's home in Books 2–3. In Book 3 the copy lives in its speakers, and knows which one Kaelin sleeps nearest

  • Elena and Daniel's home

    A flat in the Polder, where Daniel mends Vila Verde's mesh nodes and laughs at his own bad soldering

  • Jamaica Bay

    The bay behind the Rockaways peninsula, held down behind its surge barrier by a line of pump stations; the barrier's pumps fail in sequence on 6 February

  • Queens

    Real New York borough; two brothers from there volunteered for the 2089 line for the stipend

  • The Gate floor

    Inside the Battery. Its clock counts each operator's bleed score beside the debris. An override is a physical key, and only the boundary reader carries one

  • The old Gate core

    The node where the Gate began. Its breaker still holds Aur'k's override key

  • The orbital arrivals hall

    The port where Elena lands in Book 3 and Kaelin meets the real Aur'k

  • The sleepers' wards

    Wards of beds in Book 3; among the trilogy's defining images
