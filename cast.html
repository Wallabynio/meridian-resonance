Elena Vázquez

Person page

Basics41 (born 2054). The Charter, New York. Systems architect at Foresight, the Charter's predictive service, in the Spire. From the mid-2080s to 2089, a lead architect in Marcus Bellamy's lattice programme
WantSoria back. At first she calls it "the truth"
WoundHer sister died in a programme Elena designed. Elena believes she missed a risk in her own numbers. The truth is worse: her throughput design is what let them keep Soria linked after her body failed
Strength → dangerSees the pattern in any system → believes she can manage any risk alone, including other people's
FlawShe protects people by deciding for them. She keeps Daniel out "for his own good" and routes danger through his life without asking
LineShe will not take from a mind that hasn't consented. It is tested hardest when the mind is Soria's
PleasuresDry, quick, self-mocking. Loves an elegant system. Cooks for Daniel when she can't say something. Bad at sleep
VoicePrecise and compressed. Understates when frightened. Explains when she should feel
BookStarts wantingHidesChoice only she can makeEnds
1To find who is sending the warningsThat she suspects it's Soria; that she's using Daniel's meshCuts the Lantern while Soria's voice begs her not toDisgraced, out of work; Daniel's trust broken, then partly mended by asking him for help
2To stop the Gate and prove what it isHow badly she wants to talk to Soria againGoes to Mars and leaves Daniel behind; brings back the truth of the mergeStranded on Mars through the Fall; offered Soria whole
3To kill the Hollow without losing SoriaThat she's tempted to go in aloneTells Daniel instead of hiding; links in as the anchor and says goodbyeAlive, grieving, unable to ever link again

Arc. Private controller → partner who shares risk → someone who can let go.

The Cobalt Basin crew

The station is the trilogy's found family: Kaelin, Aur'k, Ines, Dae-jung, Funmi and Petra. Belonging there means safety and obligation at once. Each of them carries someone else's risk, and each is the one the others can't do without.

  • Earth night. Off shift, the crew watches Earth's weather feeds minutes late and bets on what the sky did. Kaelin always loses.
  • Across the trilogy. Book 1 loses Funmi, and Petra goes quiet. Book 2 splits the crew across two worlds and kills Ines, while Dae-jung holds the station. Book 3 brings Kaelin and Aur'k home, and Petra wakes.

The integration rule. Every irreversible choice about the Hollow changes a principal relationship, and every relationship change drives a later choice. Each book's emotional climax is the relationship choice that makes the technical action possible.
