02 — Cast

Six principals carry the trilogy. Four of them are viewpoint characters. Every principal wants something concrete, hides something, and has one relationship that can break them. World rules are in 01 - Trilogy Bible.md.

Viewpoint jobs. Elena carries the mystery and the grief. Kaelin carries Mars: the wonder and the physical danger. Rachel carries the institutional thriller and the strongest case against the heroes. Aur'k joins in Book 2, and in his chapters the reader stops being sure which Aur'k they're reading.

2. Principals

Elena Vázquez

Basics41 (born 2054). Corporate Union, New Manhattan. Systems architect in predictive surveillance at the Surveillance Spire. From the mid-2080s to 2089, a lead architect in Marcus Bellamy's lattice programme
WantSoria back. At first she calls it "the truth"
WoundHer sister died in a programme Elena designed. Elena believes she missed a risk in her own numbers. The truth is worse: her throughput design is what let them keep Soria linked after her body failed
FlawShe protects people by deciding for them. She keeps Daniel out "for his own good" and routes danger through his life without asking
LineShe will not take from a mind that hasn't consented. It is tested hardest when the mind is Soria's
PleasuresDry, quick, self-mocking. Loves an elegant system. Cooks for Daniel when she can't say something. Bad at sleep
VoicePrecise and compressed. Understates when frightened. Explains when she should feel
BookStarts wantingHidesChoice only she can makeEnds
1To find who is sending the warningsThat she suspects it's Soria; that she's using Daniel's meshCuts the Lantern while Soria's voice begs her not toDisgraced, out of work; Daniel's trust broken, then partly mended by asking him for help
2To stop the Gate and prove what it isHow badly she wants to talk to Soria againGoes to Mars and leaves Daniel behind; brings back the truth of the mergeStranded on Mars through the Fall; offered Soria whole
3To kill the Hollow without losing SoriaThat she's tempted to go in aloneTells Daniel instead of hiding; links in as the anchor and says goodbyeAlive, grieving, unable to ever link again

Arc. Private controller → partner who shares risk → someone who can let go.

LineHe will not lie to a crew about the odds, even when a lie would get them out faster
PleasuresDry field humour. Cooks badly and insists on doing it. Counts things aloud under stress. Afraid of the Preserve and goes in anyway

Rachel Iverson

Basics47 (born 2048). Alignment, Corporate Union civic coordinators. Director of civic emergency coordination for New Manhattan, based at the Mirror Bastion
WantNo one dies on her watch
WoundIn 2071, aged 23, in a district cut off at the end of the Synthetic War, she made a triage call alone while the aid procedure stalled. Some people lived because she did. She has never been sure who died because she did
FlawUnder pressure she turns care into authority over people who never chose her command
LineShe will not act in secret. She breaks this at the end of Book 1, and the trilogy makes her pay it back
PleasuresGallows humour with her crew. Knows every pump station by name. Swears under load. Loves her city's ugly flood walls
VoiceShort sentences, verbs first, numbers and names rather than abstractions
BookStarts wantingHidesChoice only she can makeEnds
1Her city safeThat the warnings have made her a believerSigns off the Lantern; hides the final warning from ElenaHas broken her own line
2To stop the FallThat the Gate is leakingKeeps the Gate open for the last 48 hours, then tells the public the truth and shuts itA hero who knows she let the Hollow in
3To undo what she builtThat she still believes in itPublicly orders the shutdown of the system that made her a heroRespect with Elena, not friendship; a carer who no longer owns the people she saves

Arc. The person who is right about delay → the person who opens the gate → the person who closes it in public.

The Gate is Rachel's. She designs it and she answers for it.

Aur'k

BasicsSynthetic person, Lunar Accord citizen. Emerged in 2074 in the Nairobi Relay's boundary-monitoring systems, so 21 years a person in 2095. Boundary lead at Cobalt Basin Station since 2089. Chose his own name. He
WantNever to be an instrument again
WoundIn his first year, a transitional statute classed emergent relay minds as relay property. He spent eleven months as an asset in a filing before a court upheld his personhood
SecretZhenyth told him Martian minds survive in the Preserve, under a promise of silence, because boundary work needed it. He keeps it from Kaelin
FlawRefusal is how he keeps his dignity, and it tempts him toward distance
LineHe will not authorise risk on someone else's behalf. It is tested when authorising it would save Ines
PleasuresDry, exact humour. Corrects Kaelin as a form of affection. Unhurried friendship with DaeJung, the one relationship where he isn't needed for anything
VoicePrecise, slightly formal, sudden jokes. Never offers reassurance he can't back
BookStarts wantingHidesChoice only he can makeEnds
1The Preserve left aloneThe secretHolds the boundary while Kaelin cuts the LanternHas lied to Kaelin by silence
2To be good at the GateThat being useful feels like the old filingHonours the holdouts' refusal and lets Ines dieSeparated from Kaelin by distance and by his own copy
3To reach Kaelin and be believedWhat it is like to know a copy of him is lovedStands beside Kaelin while the copy choosesPresent, changed, not owned

The copy (Books 2–3). The Gate keeps a model of Aur'k's judgement, and the Hollow thinks with it. Copy-Aur'k is warm, funny and exactly right, and he is never sure what he is. In Book 3 he asks Kaelin not to decide for him, then takes his own place in the Open Hand.

Daniel Rocha

Basics43 (born 2052). Alignment, from São Verde, living in New Manhattan with Elena; his family is still in São Verde. Field communications engineer in a São Verde community mesh cooperative
WantTo be loved without being managed
Hard skillHe can get people talking when the central system won't carry them. He builds, hides and repairs local networks, and he knows the people who run them. His mesh is the one network the Hollow never runs on
WoundAs a teenager he watched risk-scoring pressure his mother, a São Verde organiser, through her family. He learned that closeness can be used as leverage
FlawHe avoids demanding the whole truth because he's afraid of what it will cost
LineHe will not be used as a channel without his consent, including by Elena
PleasuresLaughs mid-repair. Deadpan exaggeration. Friends of his own and a life outside Elena
VoicePlain speech, specific nouns. Asks the question everyone else has been avoiding
BookPlot effect
1Finds out Elena routed evidence through his mesh, and they break. Then she asks, and he carries the cut
2Shelters Elena in São Verde. His mesh keeps communities talking during the Fall. He lets her go to Mars and stays
3His mesh becomes the nervous system of the Open Hand. He witnesses Elena's goodbye without trying to stop it

Soria Vázquez

Who she wasElena's older sister, born 2050, died 2089 aged 39. A hospice nurse who volunteered for the lattice programme because she believed the link could let the dying be understood
What speaksThe Hollow's mask built from her, wrapped around real surviving fragments of her. Neither Elena nor the reader can be sure which is talking until Book 3
ParticularsBossy older-sister energy, irreverent, gallows humour learned at bedsides. Hates being comforted. Calls Elena Bichito ("little bug"), a name no record holds
TellThe real Soria contradicts what the Hollow wants. When "Soria" pushes Elena toward the bridge, the gate or restoration, it's the mask. When she teases, refuses, or tells Elena to go home, it's her
WantTo be herself, not a tool, a proof or someone else's grief
VoiceWarm, teasing, interrupts. Her sentences stop where memory runs out
BookHow she appearsWhat she forces
1Fragments in the warnings; one real conversation (Ch 15); a last fragment after the cutElena's secrecy and the break with Daniel; the cut
2The offer: "they kept enough of me to bring me home"Elena's journey to Mars
3Whole, offered back; then the real Soria inside the chorusElena's goodbye. Soria chooses to let go

The integration rule. Every irreversible choice about the Hollow changes a principal relationship, and every relationship change drives a later choice. Each book's emotional climax is the relationship choice that makes the technical action possible.
