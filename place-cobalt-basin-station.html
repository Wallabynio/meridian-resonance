World · Mars · Treaty station

Cobalt Basin Station

A small station in the Hellas basin, above the Preserve; Kaelin and Aur'k's home

Set in 7 chapters Book 1 · Ch 2–19

What it is

At a glance

Is
Formally Hellas Treaty Station: a small treaty station in the Hellas basin, Mars's deepest and warmest lowland, above the Preserve. The crew call the place Cobalt Basin, for the blue of the lattice in the rock. Kaelin and Aur'k's home
Held by
The Signatories. Station command is staffed by the Continuity
How people live
A found family on shift: the crew watches Earth's weather feeds minutes late and bets on what the sky did. Its betting slate is a cargo panel by the galley
Senses
Recycled air, red dust in every seal, the cold outside the hull; a dust-storm season that closes the sky
What the heat did
It was funded by an Earth that wanted a second place to keep what it knew. Every crisis on Earth since has been a cut to its supply line
Lives on
Its supply line: water, parts and compute by ship, cheapest every 26 months. Aur'k's compute is a line in its budget
Breaks when
A transfer window is missed, or a blackout leaves nobody able to help
Whose it is
Kaelin, Aur'k, Ines, Dae-jung, Funmi, Petra
